About the Conference

Bringing together the global Datamine team to shape what comes next.

Each year, Datamine brings its global team together for a week of shared thinking, honest conversations, and decisions that shape the year ahead. This is that week — and it matters that you're there.

With team members joining from across the globe, this is one of the few moments in the year where our entire global community is in the same room. That makes it something worth showing up for.

What to Expect

Four pillars. One week.

A structured week designed to deliver clarity, alignment, and momentum for the year ahead.

01

The Year That Was

An honest look at 2026: our financial performance, what we delivered, where we fell short, and what it means for how we move forward. Transparent, grounded, and forward-facing.

02

Strategy for 2027

Our primary focus for the week. We'll work through the strategic priorities that will shape the next year: where we're investing, where we're pulling back, and how we're positioning Datamine for sustainable growth in our key markets.

03

Organic Growth

A dedicated stream exploring how we grow from within: expanding wallet share with existing customers, accelerating new logo acquisition, deepening product adoption, and building the commercial capabilities that compound over time.

04

Connecting as a Team

Beyond the sessions, this is a chance to strengthen the relationships that make a distributed global team actually function. Shared meals, informal time, and the kind of conversations that don't happen over a Teams call.

Travel Information

Everything you need to know about travelling to South Africa for the conference.

Visa Requirements

Check your visa requirements early

Many nationalities can enter South Africa visa-free for tourism purposes. However, requirements vary significantly by passport. Please check the South African Department of Home Affairs website or your nearest South African embassy for your specific requirements.

If you require a visa support letter from Datamine, please indicate this during registration and contact Anton Clowes directly.

Yellow Fever Certificate

This may be required. Check before you travel

South Africa may require proof of yellow fever vaccination if you are travelling from or transiting through a country where yellow fever is endemic. This typically includes parts of Sub-Saharan Africa and South America.

If this applies to your journey, you must carry your International Certificate of Vaccination (Yellow Card). Consult your travel doctor or local health authority at least 10 days before departure.

Airport & Transfers

The main arrival gateway is Cape Town International Airport (CPT). Most international delegates will connect through major hubs such as Dubai (DXB), Johannesburg (JNB), London Heathrow (LHR), or Amsterdam (AMS).

Airport transfer arrangements will be coordinated by the events team. Please indicate your arrival and departure details during registration so transfers can be arranged. Further details will be communicated closer to the event.

Indicate your flight details during registration to secure your transfer

South Africa Entry Requirements

When entering South Africa, ensure your passport has at least two blank pages available for immigration stamps, and that your passport is valid for a minimum of 30 days beyond your departure date.

You may be asked to provide a return or onward travel ticket and evidence of sufficient funds for your stay. Keep your conference invitation email accessible in the event of any questions at immigration.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended for all delegates
