Getting there & what to know

Everything you need

We've taken care of the details so you can focus on what matters.

Travel

Fly into Cape Town International (CPT). Direct connections from most major hubs. Shuttle arrangements from the airport will be confirmed 3 weeks before the event.

Accommodation

A room block is reserved at Pearl Valley Hotel at a group rate. Use the registration form to secure your suite. Please do not contact the hotel directly for this reservation.

Visa Support

Most EU, UK, US, Australian, and Canadian passports can enter South Africa visa-free for up to 90 days. Check requirements early. Official invitation letters available on request.

Expenses

All summit programme and evening event costs are covered by Datamine. Travel and accommodation are reimbursable — submit through the standard expense process within 30 days.

Dress Code

Smart casual throughout. Evening events may be slightly smarter. Comfortable footwear is recommended for the estate grounds and outdoor sessions.

Equipment

Bring your laptop and standard development setup. Hackathon teams will be provided with shared cloud sandbox environments. Full technical briefing sent two weeks prior.

Connectivity

High-speed Wi-Fi is available across all conference and suite areas. VPN and remote access to Datamine internal systems will work as normal throughout the week.
